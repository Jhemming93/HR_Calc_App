Marketplace vs direct trade

The better selling method depends on more than the headline price. Compare what the seller receives, what the buyer pays, how quickly the transaction can happen, and how much effort or uncertainty each method introduces.

Marketplace selling

The Marketplace gives you a visible listing that buyers can discover without arranging a one-to-one negotiation. Its main advantage is convenience: you can set a price and wait for the market to come to you. The trade-off is that seller fees can materially reduce your net proceeds.

Direct trading

A direct trade can allow the seller to negotiate the agreed Gold amount directly with a buyer. Depending on the trade system and buyer-side costs, the amount the buyer ultimately spends can be higher than the amount the seller receives. Always verify the live trade screen because game rules and fees can change.

Compare net proceeds, not just sale price

Suppose one method lets you advertise a higher number but takes a larger fee. The larger gross price may still produce less Gold in your account. Highrise Gold Lab is designed around net proceeds so that the comparison starts from what you actually keep.

Consider the buyer's total cost

A buyer may care about the total amount they spend, not just the amount you receive. If a direct-trade fee is buyer-paid, a 1,000g agreed price may cost the buyer more than 1,000g. That can affect whether your offer is competitive with Marketplace alternatives.

Liquidity can change the best choice

If an item is popular and moves quickly, Marketplace convenience may be valuable. If the item is niche, finding a specific buyer through direct negotiation may produce a better match. Neither method is automatically superior for every item.

Negotiation changes the math

Direct trades can introduce counteroffers. Decide your minimum acceptable net amount before negotiating. That keeps you from agreeing to a price that feels close to your target but actually falls below your break-even point.

Use both scenarios before deciding

Enter the same buy price and realistic sale price into the Flip calculator, then switch between Marketplace and Direct trade. Compare the seller's net proceeds, the buyer's estimated total where shown, and the maximum purchase price that still reaches your target profit.

The calculator's fee fields are editable for a reason. If the game shows a different current fee than the default, use the fee shown in game.
