Privacy policy

Highrise Gold Lab is designed to work without creating a user account. The calculator runs in your browser, and its current save feature uses browser localStorage.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Information the calculator stores

If saving is enabled through the app's Save or autosave behavior, calculator settings are stored in localStorage on your device. This can include entered prices, fees, selected calculator tab, Grab rarities, probabilities, resale values, and related preferences.

Where that saved data goes

The app's own calculator code does not send those saved settings to a Highrise Gold Lab account or application server. They remain associated with the browser/site storage on the device where you use the app. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching browsers/devices can remove or make that data unavailable.

Clearing saved data

Use the Clear saved data button in the calculator to remove the app's saved localStorage record. Browser settings can also clear site storage.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most hosting and network services, the hosting infrastructure may process routine technical information needed to deliver the site, such as network requests and security-related logs. Highrise Gold Lab does not use that information to build calculator profiles.

Advertising

Highrise Gold Lab does not currently describe advertising as active in this policy. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, this policy will be updated before or alongside activation to explain the advertising provider, cookies or similar technologies, relevant consent controls, and available choices.

Third-party links

The site may link to Highrise support pages, GitHub, or other external websites. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

Children

This site is a general-purpose calculator and does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from visitors. Do not submit personal information through public GitHub issues.

Policy changes

This page may be updated as features, hosting, analytics, or advertising change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.
