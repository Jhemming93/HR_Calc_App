About Highrise Gold Lab

Highrise Gold Lab is an independent, fan-made calculator and learning resource built to make trading math easier to understand before committing Gold.

What it does

The site calculates estimated profit, break-even prices, target-profit purchase limits, Grab expected value, and shop-resale scenarios using numbers entered by the visitor.

What it doesn't do

It does not connect to a Highrise account, scrape a live market feed, guarantee item values, or execute trades.

Why the site exists

Trading decisions often involve more than comparing two visible prices. Fees, probability, resale assumptions, and break-even points can make a large difference. The goal is to make those assumptions explicit so players can make their own decisions with clearer math.

Independent project

Highrise Gold Lab is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Highrise or its publisher. Highrise names and references are used descriptively to explain the purpose of the independent tool.

Feedback and corrections

If you find a calculation issue, accessibility problem, outdated mechanic, or unclear explanation, you can open a public issue on the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post account credentials, private messages, or other sensitive personal information there.
